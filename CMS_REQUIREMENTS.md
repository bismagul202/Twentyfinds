# TWENTYFINDS CMS requirements

Planning reference. Read alongside [BUSINESS_CONTEXT.md](BUSINESS_CONTEXT.md). Laravel and MySQL own published content and commerce state. Next.js renders the customer site. The owner uses the protected Laravel dashboard at `/admin`, and UK fulfilment staff need a separate restricted interface. The owner manages operations from Pakistan. UK stock fulfils UK customer orders. Every sellable item or variant costs £0.01–£19.99 in GBP.

## Existing implementation and gaps

| Area | Existing implementation | Required additions |
|---|---|---|
| Pages and copy | `Page`, `Banner`, `storefront_copy`; `CmsController` and `StorefrontCopyController`; draft and publish controls, page SEO title and description, scheduled banner windows, and per-field copy overrides | Page duplication, eligible deletion, scheduled pages, protected preview, revisions and restore, structured sections, and a clear editing path for every built-in route. Page overrides currently cover selected routes and `/pages/[slug]`; shop and category templates remain code-defined. |
| Storefront | `GET /api/storefront` returns public catalog, banners, published pages and copy. `cms.ts`, `ManagedContent`, `StorefrontCopy`, `ProductGrid` and the existing category, product and home layouts render the payload. Requests use `no-store`. | A reusable section renderer, focused published APIs, protected draft previews, publication-aware caching, and database-backed production marketing copy. Keep existing App Router loading, errors and server rendering. Remove production dependency on static marketing defaults only after content parity. |
| Site settings and media | Copy overrides and `site-announcement` / `site-footer` page slots. Image upload and primary selection for products, category and banner uploads, and `GET /api/media/{file}`. | Brand assets, global theme, managed navigation, centralized image and video library, folders, search, metadata, alt text, replacement, multi-select, usage and safe deletion. |
| Catalog | `Category`, `Product`, `ProductImage`, `ProductVariant`; editors for nested categories, product photos, variants, selling and previous prices. Application and database constraints protect the selling price range. | Structured attributes, collections and rules, catalog SEO, richer management and workflows for costs, promotions and wishlist analytics. |
| Commerce and fulfilment | Models and tables for inventory and movements, carts and wishlists, orders, coupons, payments, shipments, returns, refunds, addresses and notifications. | Inventory receiving and reservations, suppliers and bulk inbound shipments, customer accounts, persistent carts and wishlists, order workflows, courier and payment adapters, reconciliation and business dashboards. | Reviews and marketing | `ProductReview` and `NewsletterSubscriber` exist. The public API exposes approved rating aggregates. | Owner dashboard and permissions | Laravel Blade dashboard with owner session login, an admin role gate, per-resource editors, search, pagination and feedback. `roles`, `permissions`, pivots and `activity_logs` already exist. | Action-level backend policies, additional role scopes, actor-attributed audit history, finance/customer privacy, and a cohesive dashboard shell with shared list and form patterns. |

## Target capabilities

1. **Page lifecycle.** Create, edit, duplicate and delete eligible content pages. Manage Homepage, About, Contact, Shop, Categories, FAQ, Privacy, Terms, Shipping, Returns and future pages. Configure title, slug and SEO. Support drafts, protected preview, publish/unpublish, scheduling, revisions and restore. Enable or disable sections independently. Checkout, authentication and tracking retain their protected routes and business behavior.
2. **Visual builder.** Add, edit, duplicate, remove, reorder by drag and drop, and toggle sections. Section types include hero slider, promo banner, image with text, rich text, category grid, product grid and carousel, featured, trending, new arrivals, best sellers, Under £5/£10/£20, testimonials, customer reviews, FAQ, newsletter, video, CTA, contact info, gallery, spacer and divider. Validate typed heading, subheading, description, desktop/mobile media, buttons, links, colours, alignment, spacing, layout, order, visibility and product/category selection. Use predefined Next.js renderers and constrained rich text.
3. **Global site controls.** Manage logo, favicon, announcements, header and mobile navigation, mega menus, footer columns and links, social links, WhatsApp, contact details, copyright, promotions, colours, typography and button styles.
4. **Media.** Centralize reusable image/video uploads with backend MIME and size validation, folders, search, previews, alt text, metadata, replacements, multi-select, usage tracking and safe deletion of unused assets.
5. **Commerce administration.** Manage products, attributes, variants, images, categories, collections, stock, suppliers, bulk shipments, purchase costs, GBP prices, orders, customers, payments, COD reconciliation, shipping, returns, refunds, coupons, promotions and wishlist analytics. Keep courier and online-payment adapters modular. Offer COD only for supported services.
6. **Review moderation.** Store genuine product reviews with privacy-limited customer details, star ratings, verified-purchase indicators, images, pending/approved/rejected states, replies, abuse reports, search/filter and moderation history. Derive verified purchase from orders. Owners cannot create customer reviews or change customer ratings. A negative rating alone is never grounds for rejection.
7. **SEO.** Global defaults and per-page/product/category titles, descriptions, slugs, canonical URLs, OG images, social sharing, robots rules and structured data. Generate sitemap entries from published records. Support checked redirects for changed slugs.
8. **Marketing.** Campaigns, scheduled banners, discounts, collections, subscribers, email campaign configuration, abandoned-cart events, analytics and pixel settings. Gate marketing events and provider scripts on recorded consent.
9. **Forms.** Build forms with configurable field types, required rules and server validation. Store submissions with status, send email notifications, add spam controls, and restrict CSV exports to authorized roles.
10. **Business reporting.** Sales and revenue, expenses, profit and loss, product and inventory performance, suppliers, international shipping and UK fulfilment costs. Record original currencies and applied exchange rates for PKR sourcing and GBP reporting. Account for purchase, freight, customs, packaging, delivery, payment fees, tax, refunds and marketing. Keep VAT, registration and tax configuration flexible for the operating business.
11. **Users and audit.** Support owner, content manager, store manager, fulfilment staff and customers. Enforce actions (view/create/edit/delete/approve/publish) in Laravel. Scope fulfilment to stock, assigned orders, dispatch, physical returns and eligible COD records. Keep supplier costs, profit reports, settings and admin permissions private. Log actors and changes on sensitive actions.
12. **Frontend contract.** Publish only public fields through Laravel REST. Keep previews authenticated, short-lived, uncached and excluded from search. Render content responsively on the server with loading/error states. Publishing must refresh the relevant Next.js content without redeploying. Keep prices, inventory, identity, checkout and payment decisions in business services.
13. **Dashboard UX.** Responsive sidebar and overview, global search, breadcrumbs, notifications, reusable tables and forms, pagination/filtering, bulk actions, confirmations and meaningful success, error, empty and loading states.
